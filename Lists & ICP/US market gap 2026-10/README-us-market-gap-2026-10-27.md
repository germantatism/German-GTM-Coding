## 6. Yuno inside view, in five lines

ACP means OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol; Yuno's Agentic Commerce product (catalog sync, certified endpoints, payments inside ChatGPT and other assistants) carries no live or beta label and no named merchants yet, while the MCP servers, Agent Toolkit, Claude and ChatGPT connectors, Payments Concierge, Maia and NOVA are live. Orchestration is mature and API-driven (conditional and smart routing, retries and fallback, monitors with auto failover, network tokens, account updater, 3DS, portable PCI L1 vault, no-code connections). Reconciliation has a new engine (settlements, fees, payouts, API reports), list price $2,500 a month plus $0.03 per transaction, and no public live customer. The subscriptions engine is documented end to end; the 16 merchants / 135K subs / 83 to 86% figure is internal only. Publicly namable US customers are GoFundMe and Whop (plus Uber by logo and Open English by story); SpaceX, Allegiant, Kraken, Automattic, Skool, xAI and Fever are internal names. Glean was unreachable all session, so nothing internal from Glean is included.
