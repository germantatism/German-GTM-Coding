**Derived per-fan math [ESTIMATES]:**
- FY2025: about $7.9B gross (derived above) / 132M active fan accounts (Ledger C7) = about $60 per active fan per year, about $5 per month.
- FY2024: $7.22B / 377.5M total fan accounts = about $19 per fan account per year (total accounts, not active, so not comparable with FY2025).
- Per-transaction ATV: with 41% of spend in subscriptions at an assumed $7 to $15 per charge and 59% in PPV and tips at an assumed $5 to $20, a blended ATV in the range of roughly $8 to $20 is plausible. This is an assumption-driven range, not a sourced figure. The May 2026 brief used $15 to $25 [ESTIMATE]; the lower bound here is more conservative. Implied transaction count at $7.9B: about 400M (at $20) to about 1B (at $8) per year [INFERENCE].
