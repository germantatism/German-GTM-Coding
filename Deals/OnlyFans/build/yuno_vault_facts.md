# Yuno token vault fact base for the OnlyFans RFP deck

Prepared 8 Oct 2026 for German Tatis and Samuel Vieira. Internal. Every Yuno claim below was re-read on 8 Oct 2026 on docs.y.uno or y.uno; the URL and a short quote sit next to each row. Labels follow Samuel's system: CONFIRMED (docs.y.uno, or company figures on y.uno), BETA (depends on the PCI Proxy, beta and enabled per organization), OPEN (not confirmed in writing, owner named), NOT IN DOCS (gap in the public reference). Internal Slack or Drive facts are marked INTERNAL and must not reach the deck.

Reading rule: a docs page proves a capability is documented, not that it is switched on for a given organization. Network tokens, the cryptogram API, the PCI Proxy, account updater and PCI data retrieval are each enabled per organization by the KAM or support.
