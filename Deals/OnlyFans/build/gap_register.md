# Gap register: the questions a careful vault buyer asked last time, checked against Yuno's public docs

Prepared 8 Oct 2026 for German Tatis and Samuel Vieira. Internal. Source of the questions: a prior standalone-vault evaluation (four rounds of buyer questions and Yuno's draft answers). Questions are rephrased generically; no customer is named. "What Yuno answered then" is what the draft said, not a confirmed fact. Verification column: CONFIRMED (docs.y.uno or y.uno, read 8 Oct 2026, URL given), BETA where the answer depends on the PCI Proxy, OPEN (not confirmed in writing, owner named), NOT IN DOCS. Risk is judged for OnlyFans' stated design: US balances first, several banks and issuers per creator, banking as a service next.

## Proposed slide content: "The questions a careful vault buyer asks, answered before you ask"

Two slides. Slide A keeps Samuel's seven rows verbatim and adds one; slide B adds seven more. Kicker: "01 · Vault · what an evaluation turns on". Headline on both: "The questions a careful vault buyer asks first, answered before you ask." Every added row maps to an existing open item or to a new one; if Samuel accepts rows 8, 9 and 15, open item 5 gains "subprocessor list" and open item 4 gains "NTID export and scheme activation", so the count of six open items does not change.

### Slide A (rows 1 to 7 verbatim from Samuel's slide 8, row 8 new)

| # | The question | Our answer | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Can we store a card without charging it? | Yes. SDK enrollment creates a vaulted token without a charge. Zero-amount verification is optional where the provider supports it. | CONFIRMED |
| 2 | Can we send a stored card to an endpoint we choose? | Yes. The forward proxy sends it to any HTTPS host on your allowlist, including providers Yuno does not connect to. | CONFIRMED BETA |
| 3 | What happens to the CVV? | It is never stored. Per the proxy guide it is held briefly after entry, 3 hours by default, for a first charge. | CONFIRMED BETA |
| 4 | Can we charge with network tokens on our own processor and stay out of PCI scope? | Yes. Cryptogram-only mode (Visa, Mastercard; enabled per organization) needs no PCI certification: it returns no card data and the proxy fills in the token. Recurring charges add the network transaction ID. | CONFIRMED BETA |
| 5 | Whose name are network tokens registered in? | Our API reference says Yuno holds the token requestor ID. Whether requestor IDs can be registered in your name is open, per network. Written answer with the RFP response. | OPEN |
| 6 | How do we leave? | A documented card export: a written request, the recipient's PCI DSS attestation, and a PGP-encrypted file over SFTP. | CONFIRMED |
| 7 | What uptime do you commit to, and how does it fail over? | You get a contractual figure and the failover design with the RFP response, not a number on a slide. | OPEN |
| 8 | What is in the export file, and what is not? | Cardholder name, card number, expiry and the Yuno token, as documented. Network transaction IDs and network tokens are not in the documented file; we scope them with you in writing. | CONFIRMED, OPEN (Trust & Vault product, Legal) |

Speaker notes addition for slide A: "Row 7: our published SLA annex at y.uno/sla states 99.90% monthly availability and a 5-minute P1 response; the figure that binds is the one in your contract. Row 8: the export process is public; the file contents are listed on the same page, so you can check what you would take with you before signing."
