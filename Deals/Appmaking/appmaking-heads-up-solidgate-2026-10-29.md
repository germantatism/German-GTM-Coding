This is not a follow-up on the proposal. It is something I thought you should see given how much of your volume runs through Solidgate.

In June the FTC filed a case against fifteen companies tied to Genesis Tech over their subscription practices, and a federal court temporarily halted their operations. Solidgate is not named in the case, but it came out of the Genesis group, so it is worth asking them directly whether their US acquiring and wallet relationships are affected in any way. With the US at around 40% of your revenue, the real question is concentration. If a US acquirer or wallet tightens on that channel, where does the traffic go that same week? That is exactly the role you described for a third layer when we spoke: your own MIDs, your own tokens, a provider-agnostic layer outside the flow of funds, in place before you need it.
