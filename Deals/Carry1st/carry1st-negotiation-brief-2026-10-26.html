5. How to defend it: four arguments

  1. We already moved. "You flagged the fixed costs and we came back with a ramp: $10,000 off the first year, all of it in the months where volume is lowest." It's the answer to his ask, not a discount.
  2. Nothing per transaction until $500K a month. Through the whole launch, he pays only the ramped platform fee.
  3. Against his margin it's small. 47 bps is about 3% of a 15% take rate (our estimate; ask for the real one). Talk in cents per purchase: 4.7¢ on $10.
  4. One lever pays for it. In our model, processing leverage is 25 to 75 bps. At $4.5M, 25 bps = $11,250, more than half the Yuno bill. These are estimates; his EBANX quote replaces them.
