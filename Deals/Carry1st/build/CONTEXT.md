Owner: German Tatis (Senior AE, Yuno, german.tatis@y.uno). Deal lead on Yuno's side: Justo Benetti (CRO). Juan Pablo Ortega (CEO) booked the review.

## The deal in six lines
- Carry1st: Africa's largest mobile-games publisher and fintech (Cape Town, founded 2018). Backed by a16z, BITKRAFT, Google, Sony Innovation Fund. Publishes and distributes games (partners include Supercell, Activision) and runs the **Carry1st Shop** (game top-ups and digital gift cards).
- Buyer: **Cordel** (exec lead, cordel@carry1st.com) and **Fatih** (technical/product side).
- What they are buying: payments for the Carry1st Shop in **Latin America**, live by **Black Friday, 27 Nov 2026** (six weeks). **Brazil first**, then Argentina, Mexico, Bolivia, Colombia; all five together if scoped in two to three weeks. No LatAm entities today, so launch runs through a **merchant-of-record provider**; non-resident accounts and local entities later (Phase 2).
- Numbers: **$4 to 5M/month in LatAm by end 2027** (Cordel's estimate, not aligned internally at Carry1st). Avg ticket ~$10, median ~$5, so ~400 to 500K tx/month at scale. Launch volume is unknown; Cordel owes written volumes by country. Yuno segmentation: gaming = M3 margin; V3 by volume at scale (V2 at launch).
- The decision: **EBANX direct** (existing relationship, perceived fastest path) vs **EBANX and others through Yuno** (EBANX primary MoR, dLocal second MoR). That comparison is the entire business case.
- Review: **Fri 16 Oct 2026, 7:30 to 8:00am PT**, Google Meet booked by JP; attendees German, Cordel, Justo. Deliverables owed to Carry1st: prototype on Yuno SDK (sandbox demo exists), checkout examples, Moon Active test access, proposal, business case.

## What was said in the discovery call (German's summary, 15 Oct 2026)
- Carry1st wants to launch its Shop (gaming top-ups and digital gift cards) in Latin America by Black Friday. Brazil could launch first, then Argentina, Mexico, Bolivia and Colombia. Cordel is open to launching all five together if payments, localization and customer support can be scoped within two to three weeks. **This is separate from the existing Saudi/EBANX collaboration** (which stays out of the deck).
- Yuno discussed providing one payments integration, local provider negotiations and checkout expertise. Juan Pablo also offered localization support if the partnership moves forward. He proposed starting with a merchant-of-record model, then evaluating non-resident accounts and local entities as volumes grow.
- **Cordel's main asks:** (1) **Demonstrate the economics**: show how processing savings, improved conversion and reduced engineering work justify Yuno's additional fee. (2) **Make pricing work for small purchases**: average ticket ~$10, median closer to $5, so fixed transaction fees are especially sensitive. (3) **Clarify the EBANX arrangement**: how working with Yuno fits alongside EBANX, including starting through a particular provider or integrating directly.
- **Fatih's asks:** checkout demos, and a comparison of merchant of record versus local presence covering launch timelines and longer-term customer experience.
- **Next steps agreed:** JP briefs Mauricio (Schwartzmann) and confirms whether Mauricio or Alfredo (Sarria) leads the opportunity · Yuno delivers a Carry1st Shop prototype on Yuno's SDK, working Moon Active test access, and additional checkout videos/examples · Cordel sends written volume projections (his initial estimate $4 to 5M/month in LatAm by end 2027, still to be aligned with his team) · Yuno prepares the commercial proposal and business case showing total payment costs, the impact of fixed fees on small tickets, and the proposed launch approach · JP books the 30-minute follow-up (now Fri 16 Oct, 7:30am PT) to review demos and proposal, confirm launch countries and assign implementation owners.
- Pricing, provider selection and final launch scope remain open.

## Justo's rules for the business case (non-negotiable)
1. Why Yuno and why not EBANX direct.
2. Retries, options (second provider), MDR reduction.
3. Numbers tied to a **low transaction count** at launch; the $7.5K is covered early.
4. Cost as a **percentage of TPV**, not a fixed per-transaction fee (a $5 median ticket makes fixed fees look terrible).
5. **$7.5K/month platform commitment with a block of transactions included**, usage above as % of TPV.
