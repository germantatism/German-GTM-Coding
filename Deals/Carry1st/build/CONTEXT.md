## The deal in six lines
- Carry1st: Africa's largest mobile-games publisher and fintech (Cape Town, founded 2018). Backed by a16z, BITKRAFT, Google, Sony Innovation Fund. Publishes and distributes games (partners include Supercell, Activision) and runs the **Carry1st Shop** (game top-ups and digital gift cards).

- What they are buying: payments for the Carry1st Shop in **Latin America**, live by **Black Friday, 27 Nov 2026** (six weeks). **Brazil first**, then Argentina, Mexico, Bolivia, Colombia; all five together if scoped in two to three weeks. No LatAm entities today, so launch runs through a **merchant-of-record provider**; non-resident accounts and local entities later (Phase 2).
- Numbers: **$4 to 5M/month in LatAm by end 2027** (Cordel's estimate, not aligned internally at Carry1st). Avg ticket ~$10, median ~$5, so ~400 to 500K tx/month at scale. Launch volume is unknown; Cordel owes written volumes by country. Yuno segmentation: gaming = M3 margin; V3 by volume at scale (V2 at launch).
