### Publishing

Esports layer: Carry1st Africa Cup (CODM World Championship qualifier) with Spotify (16 Jul 2025) and Mentos (15 Aug 2025); Mobile Legends all-female tournament (26 Mar 2025).

### Carry1st Shop
* What it sells: in-game currency, passes, subscriptions and vouchers for games, dating apps and lifestyle services (carry1st.com); brands in key finding 8.
* Channels: web (shop.carry1st.com), iOS and Android app ("Carry1st Shop · Vouchers", seller ADG Technology Inc., version 2.18.3), WhatsApp shop in Nigeria and South Africa (Carry1st blog).
* Delivery: "Instant topup & delivery"; Supercell accounts "automatically topped up, no vouchers, pins, or hassle".
* Loyalty: Discount Points (1 Aug 2024); storefront shows per-SKU discounts of 2% to 8% (e.g., Xbox gift card US "7% Off").
* LatAm SKUs already in the catalog: "Free Fire Diamonds (LATAM)", "Free Fire Diamonds Brazil" (US storefront sitemap, 15 Oct 2026). Prices and valid countries do not render; OPEN, owner German: ask Cordel which LatAm SKUs are live today and through which rail.

### Pay1st (one paragraph, context only)
Carry1st's proprietary payments solution for Africa and the Middle East: "manages all compliance, tax, foreign exchange, and risk" so the merchant can "collect in USD without any administrative burden"; 120+ local methods (Pay1st page) or 200+ (homepage); six named countries; integration via gateway (web store, game client, APK), Shop listing, or API with "dedicated engineering support". It explains why Carry1st is comfortable with a merchant-of-record model and why LatAm, where Pay1st has no rails, needs EBANX or dLocal.

### Expansion statements
* Africa and the Middle East only, in every 2025 to 2026 public statement found (release 25 Aug 2026; job posts; Stellar Gate Games MENA partnership 16 Jan 2025).
