# Prompt Claude Design: replace the CellPoint pricing slide with two side-by-side options (2026-09-01)

Target: the deck **"Proposal - CellPoint Digital + Yuno"** (the one attached to this prompt / already open in the canvas). Edit the **"Proposal · White label partnership"** slide (the pricing slide right after the "Proposal" section divider, currently laid out as three cards: Platform Fee $50,000/month, Success Fee $0.07/successful transaction, Revenue Share 50/50). Edit this slide directly, do not touch the "Why Yuno?" section before it or the closing slide after it.

## Why this slide is wrong now

On the September 1, 2026 alignment call, CellPoint's Andrew Goddard (Chief Strategy & Product Officer) stepped back from the full white-label deal this slide describes. His reason: CellPoint has 12+ years of shareholder capital invested in their own orchestration engine ("Velocity," run out of their India dev center), and replacing that engine with Yuno's white-label platform complicates the asset-value story they need for their shareholders (Tosca Penta/Toscafund). He was explicit that this isn't dead, just paused for internal review with their CEO.

What he asked for instead, as an interim step: just the APM connections, not a takeover of their orchestration core. Yuno's CRO Justo Benetti confirmed on the call this can be sold as standalone components: APM/payment method connections, 3DS orchestration, account updater, standalone poll. That is the actual scope of this new proposal. This is not a scaled-down white label, it's a different, narrower offer, so the slide's framing needs to change, not just the numbers.

## What to build

**Reframe the section title and subtitle.** Move away from "White label partnership" language (that implies the full engine takeover Andrew just paused). Something like "Proposal · APM Connectivity Partnership" or "Proposal · Payment Connections, Your Way to Market Faster" fits better. Subtitle should set up that there are two ways to structure it, e.g. "One platform fee, two ways to structure the upside, pick the model that fits your reseller economics."

**Replace the three-card layout with two cards, side by side, for direct comparison.** Both options share the same platform fee; they differ only in how the upside is structured.

**Option 1 — Revenue Share**
- Platform fee: **$15,000 / month**
- Revenue share: **50/50 on net platform revenue**, across all transactions that route through the APM connections Yuno provides
- No per-transaction fee, no integration fee

**Option 2 — Integration Fee**
- Platform fee: **$15,000 / month**
- Integration fee: **$500 per new APM / payment method integration**
- No revenue share, no per-transaction fee

Under or above both cards, a shared band explaining what the $15,000/month platform fee covers for either option (this part is identical in both, don't repeat it twice):
- A dedicated strategy team (CAM/TAM) supporting CellPoint along the way
- Access to 1,200+ payment connections
- Maintenance: the cost of keeping those connections live

Visually: keep the deck's existing card language (rounded panels, one card in the filled/blue treatment to draw the eye, dollar figures large per the current $50,000 and $0.07 cards), but make the two options feel like a real side-by-side comparison, not three unrelated blocks. A shared "what's included" strip underneath both cards, plus a light visual divider or "OR" marker between the two cards, would read clearly.

**Replace the bottom "A complete suite · four pillars · one brain" band.** That band currently describes the full white-label OS (checkout & SDKs, subscription engine, NOVA AI, full four-pillar stack) — that's the wrong scope for this narrower offer, since CellPoint keeps their own orchestration and front end. Replace it with a lighter "what's included in the connections" band scoped to what Justo actually confirmed on the call: APM / payment method connections (1,200+), 3DS orchestration, account updater, standalone poll, plus the dedicated CAM/TAM team. Do not carry over checkout SDKs, subscription engine, NOVA AI, or reconciliation/ledger claims into this slide, they belong to the full white-label pitch, not this one.

**Drop the "3-year contract term" footer line entirely**, or replace it with a neutral placeholder like "Term: to be confirmed." That 3-year figure was tied to the full white-label deal; nothing on the call set a term for this lighter connections-only model, so don't carry it forward as if it were agreed.

## One number to flag, not silently fix

The $15,000/month fee is described as covering "1,200+ payment connections." The rest of this same deck (the "Why Yuno?" stat tiles) uses "1,500+ integrations" and "1,000+ payment methods." These are three different numbers in one deck. Use **1,200+** on this slide exactly as given, since that's the number tied to this specific pricing tier, but don't try to reconcile it against the other stat tiles elsewhere in the deck, that number needs a human decision first, not an automatic fix.

## What NOT to do

- Don't keep any reference to "$50,000/month," "$0.07 per successful transaction," or "revenue share on every deal CellPoint Digital closes" as a single unified structure, that whole three-card framing is replaced, not adjusted.
- Don't describe this as CellPoint's full orchestration engine being replaced or as a white-label takeover, Andrew specifically paused that framing on the call.
- Don't invent an integration count, a transaction volume estimate, or a contract term that wasn't stated.
- Match the deck's existing visual system exactly (same fonts, same blue palette, same card and section-divider style used throughout). This is a content and structure fix on one slide, not a redesign of the deck.
