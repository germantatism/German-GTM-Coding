Build one 16:9 pricing slide (1920x1080): **"Pricing"**, for the commercial proposal **FlightHub + Yuno**. Use the attached Flair pricing slide image as the exact visual reference: replicate its layout, typography and color system, only the content changes. All figures in USD, monthly billing, copy in English.

## Visual system (match the reference exactly)

- Background: light lavender grey (#E9E9F3). Ink text (#20222A). Yuno blue (#3B4BF9) for fees and accents.
- "⠿ yuno" wordmark top right in Yuno blue, thin horizontal divider line under the header area, small "Pricing" page label.
- Two-column layout: left column for the transaction fee tranches, right column for the platform fee and the pending items.

2. Below the card, a stacked list of three rows in the same row style as the reference (white rows, product name left, value right), under a small header **"Pending scoping"**:

## What NOT to do

- Do not add a minimum monthly billing line, a smart routing row, a NOVA AI row, a monthly/yearly projection table, or a contract term. This proposal is only the platform fee, the pay-in tranches, and the pending list. Nothing else.
- Do not invent prices for Reconciliations, 3DS, or Network Tokens. They stay as "Pending" with no number.
- Do not change, round, or reformat the numbers given above.
- Do not add any claims about FlightHub, volumes, savings, or approval rates.
- Do not use em-dashes anywhere in the copy.
