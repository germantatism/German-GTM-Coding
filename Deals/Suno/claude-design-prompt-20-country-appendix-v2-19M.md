## WHY THE NUMBERS CHANGED

The underlying Business Case assumptions were raised (Δ acceptance rate, incremental transaction %, TPV-to-APM shift %, take rate), moving the total portfolio benefit from $12.06M/year to **$19.19M/year** (confirmed live in Google Sheet `1lIjBmQvp5dUq5gaQI1x2vfOV12htAwTV8iE3d6K057g`, tab RESUMEN). Of the 20 appendix countries, **14 are directly modeled** in the BC Pay-ins tabs (real Cards Benefit + APMs Benefit, unscaled) and **6 are not modeled** (Italy, Turkey, Poland, Ukraine, Netherlands, Vietnam) — those 6 are scaled from their old $12M-era appendix values by the same portfolio growth factor (×1.7845).

**The 20 countries total $14.466M/year, not $19.19M.** That is correct and intentional: the 14 modeled countries only capture ~64% of Suno's total web traffic (281M annual visits). The remaining ~36% (long-tail markets outside this top-20 view) accounts for the gap to the full portfolio total. Do not force the appendix total to equal $19M — say "20 of Suno's top markets, representing the large majority of the addressable opportunity" rather than "the full $19M."
