## Reply for Sean to send to Phil Miller / procurement@bcferries.com

Hi Phil,

Thanks for the reply and the pointer to Section 1.1.4. We'll register on BC Bid and respond.

One point we'd like to put in front of you before questions close on the 28th. The NRFP is built as processing, gateway, fraud and wallets, which reads as four products bought as one processor-led stack. The outcome in your scope, reliable and cost-effective acceptance across every channel with the flexibility to modernize later, is what an orchestration layer delivers: one integration above the processors, so BC Ferries can route, fail over and add methods or providers without re-procuring the stack. It also means a single processor outage, like the fleetwide one in September 2023, doesn't take every channel down with it.

We would respond as a Proponent Team, with an acquiring partner on processing and Yuno on gateway, fraud and wallets. Before we commit the proposal to that structure, could we get 30 minutes with you and the payments owner, arranged through Procurement, to confirm it fits the Core Solution evaluation? If the process doesn't allow a call at this stage, we'll submit the question formally before the deadline and build the proposal around it either way.

Thanks,
Sean
