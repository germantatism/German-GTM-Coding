### How Yuno Can Help Fareportal

**Consumer-side checkout is the one unrouted layer.** Fareportal has already orchestrated the *supplier* side — the Amadeus Outpayce B2B Wallet went live Jan 2026 for virtual-card supplier settlement — and even markets "payment orchestration" through its Enterprise arm. So the pitch is never "you lack an orchestrator." It's that the *consumer* checkout across CheapOair and OneTravel (USD, CAD and other currencies, high-value air tickets on fraud-scored travel MCCs) runs with no publicly evident consumer-side acquirer redundancy or smart routing. Air ticketing is one of the highest-decline card categories; Yuno sits on top of the current setup, adds acquirer failover, and routes each transaction to the best-performing acquirer per market for a double-digit acceptance uplift. Best-fit proof: **McDonald's runs Yuno across 18 countries for +4.7% acceptance and $3.2M incremental revenue** — the multi-country, multi-currency acceptance case that maps onto Fareportal's footprint.

**Charge-integrity and reconciliation tied to live legal exposure.** Fareportal's most documented consumer-side pain is charge accuracy: an active Quebec class action alleges customers were charged more than the displayed "final total price" across CheapOair and OneTravel, on top of a 2022 $2.6M NY AG settlement over deceptive checkout/fee patterns and a high volume of duplicate-charge and slow-refund complaints. Underneath sits Affirm, PayPal and cards across multiple brands and currencies with no single reconciliation or transaction view. Yuno unifies acquiring, APMs, BNPL and disputes under one control plane with consistent charge/settle logic and a single per-transaction view — directly attacking the duplicate-charge, reconciliation and refund-latency problems surfacing in both the litigation and the complaints. Best-fit proof: **Rappi runs new providers on Yuno with zero implementation time and cut analyst issue-resolution time by 80%**; Livelo's 50% transaction recovery is a strong secondary reference.

**Local-method gap in emerging markets (verify before leading with it).** OneTravel's fastest-growing traffic may be heavily India-weighted — one internal brief cited ~46% India, though current SimilarWeb data instead shows OneTravel as US-dominant (~70%) with India not in the top 5. **[INFERENCE / DISPUTED — confirm the market mix before pitching India.]** If India (or any emerging market) is in fact a material and growing share, a card-only flow is leaking conversion, because the majority of Indian digital volume clears on UPI/RuPay/netbanking, not international cards, and cross-border card success into India is structurally low. Yuno adds local acquiring and local methods through one integration in weeks. Best-fit proof: **inDrive live across 10 markets in <8 months at a ~90% approval rate** — the emerging-market, high-approval outcome. Lead with the two angles above; use this one only once the traffic mix is confirmed.
