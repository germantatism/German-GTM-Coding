## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Acebet is a two-entity iGaming group that launched in late 2025. acebet.co (acebet.com redirects there) is the international real-money crypto plus fiat casino and sportsbook, owned by Golden Frog Inc. under Anjouan registration and licence number 15831, with payments "managed by" its Cyprus subsidiary 51 Plus One Ltd. acebet.cc is a US sweepstakes casino run by Trey Mark Services Limited (Cyprus) on the same codebase, and it is the volume business: roughly 6.7M monthly visits versus 371K for the international site (SimilarWeb, Sep-2026).

Key payment-stack finding: the platform is proprietary and hand-wired. The code bundle exposes at least ten separately integrated rails (Fireblocks and Mesh for crypto, a card and wallet PSP keyed "breeze", a cards/Interac/PIX/Open Banking PSP keyed "paytech", peer.xyz for Revolut and Wise, Kinguin gift cards, Zen and other USD deposit rails, PayPal and MassPay payouts, a LatAm and India payout vendor keyed "tx-tech", Whop rails on the US site) behind provider-branded hosted redirects, with Hub88 for casino content, Betby for sports, Sumsub for KYC, Sift for fraud, and no orchestrator anywhere in the code. Roughly 60% of the 500+ Trustpilot reviews sampled are about withdrawals, blocked cash-outs or KYC delays.

Yuno opportunity: one integration replacing a growing set of point rails, payout routing plus real-time monitoring to fix the withdrawal complaints, local acquiring where a Cyprus entity processes cross-border today, and stablecoin rails for a crypto-native player base. Fit caveat: the operating licence is Anjouan, and Yuno legal has historically required a local licence for high-risk merchants (see Internal Context). Confirm appetite with #salesops-legal before pricing anything.
