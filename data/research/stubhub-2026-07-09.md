# SDR Research Brief — StubHub (STUB)
*Yuno Payment Orchestrator · Framework v8.0 · 2026-07-09*

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**StubHub Holdings, Inc.** (NYSE: STUB; New York; CEO/founder Eric Baker) is a global two-sided secondary ticket marketplace that operates the **StubHub** brand in North America and **viagogo** internationally, together serving **200+ countries, 30+ languages and 45+ currencies** on **$9.2B GMV (FY2025)**. No payment orchestrator was found; the business runs a fragmented multi-currency, multi-brand, dual-flow stack (buyer pay-ins across cards, PayPal, wallets and BNPL via Afterpay/Klarna/Zip, plus seller payouts via PayPal/Payoneer/bank transfer). The two loudest pain surfaces are **seller-payout delays** (a structural complaint, and partly a deliberate float strategy that earned ~$41M in interest income) and a fresh **FTC $10M drip-pricing settlement (April 2026)**. Post-IPO margin and approval-rate scrutiny, a cross-border 45-currency footprint with no orchestration layer, and unified pay-in + payout needs make StubHub a **🔴 high-priority** orchestration and payouts opportunity.
