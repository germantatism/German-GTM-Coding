*Framework v8.0 — 4 agents, accuracy-first | Compiled 2026-08-24*

> **Target clarification:** "vix" was researched as **ViX** (vix.com), the Spanish-language AVOD/SVOD/FAST streaming service owned by **TelevisaUnivision**, launched Jan 2022. Not to be confused with Wix.com (website builder) or the CBOE VIX volatility index. Note: TelevisaUnivision does **not** appear connected to the separate Paramount/Skydance merger (completed Aug 2025) — no evidence found linking the two entities [confirmed absence, not found = not linked].

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

ViX is TelevisaUnivision's Spanish-language streaming platform, now the fastest-growing major SVOD service in the Americas (10M+ global subscribers, profitable for the first time in FY2025 on $4.8B parent-company revenue). Its traffic is heavily concentrated in Mexico (45–71% depending on measurement window) with the US, Peru, and Colombia as secondary markets. The payment stack is fragmented across **at least 7 distinct billing rails** — direct card billing, 5+ app-store/device billers (Apple, Google, Samsung, Roku, Amazon, Vizio), Verizon carrier billing (US), and Mexico-specific OXXO Pay/gift cards — with **no confirmed PSP, gateway, or orchestrator** identified anywhere in public sources. This fragmentation correlates with a heavy, well-documented pattern of double-billing and post-cancellation charge complaints across Trustpilot (1.3–1.8★), PissedConsumer, and ViX's own help center (which has a dedicated "I was double charged" article). Combined with 2026 price increases and continued expansion into new US distribution deals (YouTube TV, DIRECTV, Charter) that add more third-party billing relationships, ViX is a strong-fit prospect for Yuno's reconciliation, smart routing, and unified billing-visibility value proposition — though no named payments decision-maker was identified, so the outreach entry point remains unconfirmed.

## SECTION 10 — Strategic Insights

**Insight #1: Billing fragmentation across 7+ rails correlates with a well-documented, systemic double-billing/cancellation complaint pattern**
Evidence: Section 3A (no confirmed PSP; card + 5 app-store rails + carrier + OXXO/gift cards) and Section 5 (Trustpilot 1.3–1.8★, dozens of dated double-charge/post-cancellation-billing reports, ViX's own "I was double charged" support article).
Pain Point: Customers can't get billing disputes resolved when purchased via a third-party store — ViX explicitly disclaims responsibility, pushing customers to their bank or the third party.
Yuno Value Prop: Unified transaction visibility and reconciliation across payment rails; real-time monitors that catch anomalies (like duplicate charges) in milliseconds rather than the multi-week support response times reported.
Best Case: Rappi — real-time monitors cut analyst resolution time by 80%.
Outreach Angle: Frame around reducing support/chargeback burden and protecting subscriber trust at a moment of active price increases, not around "fixing broken payments."

**Insight #2: No confirmed PSP or orchestrator despite $1.8B in subscription/licensing revenue and 10M+ global subscribers**
Evidence: Section 3A/3B — static site fetch found no PSP script signatures; no orchestration vendor mentioned anywhere in public sources.
Pain Point: At this scale, a legacy or in-house billing stack without a visibility/orchestration layer is a plausible explanation for the reconciliation issues in Section 5.
Yuno Value Prop: No-code PSP enablement and new-market rollout in weeks, not months — relevant given ViX's active US distribution expansion (Insight #5).
Best Case: InDrive — live in 10 LatAm markets in under 8 months with 90% approval rate.
Outreach Angle: Position Yuno as infrastructure for scale, not a fix for something broken.

**Insight #3: Mexico is the dominant market (45–71% of traffic) but confirmed cash/voucher APM coverage is limited to OXXO Pay + physical gift cards**
Evidence: Section 1 (traffic dominance) + Section 4A (only OXXO Pay/gift cards confirmed as non-card MX methods).
Pain Point: [INFERENCE — not confirmed] Possible gap vs. broader Mexican APM landscape (e.g., SPEI bank transfer), but this is NOT verified and must not be asserted as a gap in outreach — frame as "worth exploring" only.
Yuno Value Prop: Smart Routing + rapid APM enablement in an already-dominant market.
Outreach Angle: Ask, don't assert — invite a conversation about Mexico payment mix rather than claiming a missing method.

**Insight #4: 2026 price increases raise the cost of every duplicate-charge or failed-cancellation incident**
Evidence: Section 7 (monthly plan +$1, MX annual +200 MXN, Roku +$8.99) layered on Section 5's complaint volume.
Pain Point: Price-sensitive subscribers who are also experiencing billing friction are a churn risk at exactly the moment ViX needs retention most.
Yuno Value Prop: 50% transaction recovery + approval uplift reduce both failed-payment churn and support load simultaneously.
Best Case: Livelo — +5% approval, 50% recovery.

**Insight #5: US expansion via YouTube TV, DIRECTV, and Charter adds more third-party billing relationships ViX doesn't control**
Evidence: Section 6 (distribution deals) + Section 3A (ViX's own disclaimer that third-party-billed subscriptions are outside its dispute-resolution scope).
Pain Point: Every new distribution partner is a new billing rail with its own reconciliation blind spot.
Yuno Value Prop: Centralized monitoring layer that gives finance/ops visibility across direct + partner billing, even where ViX doesn't own the underlying PSP relationship.
Outreach Angle: Tie to the CFO transition (Juan Pablo Newman, Sep 2024) — new finance leadership is a natural moment to evaluate payment infrastructure.
