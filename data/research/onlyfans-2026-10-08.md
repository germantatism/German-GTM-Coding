## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

OnlyFans is a $1.55B net-revenue creator platform (FY2025, year to 30 Nov 2025) that paid $6.3B to creators in the year and runs with 47 employees; its audited FY2025 accounts list receivables from "Payment Service Providers" in the plural, but no OnlyFans document names a processor, an orchestrator or a vault vendor. Pay-in is web-only, cards plus an OnlyFans Wallet, charged in USD everywhere; payouts depend on where the creator lives, go by bank wire outside the US, and bank account data for direct transfers is held by OnlyFans itself. Architect Capital bought about 16% for $535M in May 2026 and its founder, now on the board, says the plan is to add financial products for creators and help them with "payment processors and banks". The Yuno opportunity is the token vault RFP OnlyFans has opened (known through our contact, not public) and the layer above it: one vault for cards, bank accounts and identity data, routing across several processors and banks, and the payout side of a multi-bank creator account.

## SECTION 10 · Strategic Insights

**Insight #1: The vault under creator banking**
Evidence: Section 6 rows 1 to 3 and 6 (Architect stake, Sagan's plan, private token vault RFP); Section 9 (cards held by processors, bank data held by OnlyFans). | Pain Point: a creator-banking product adds bank accounts, identity records, issued cards and wallet credentials to a business whose card data today lives with its processors; a card-only vault will be re-evaluated within a year. | Yuno Value Prop: one PCI DSS Level 1 vault for cards and bank accounts, a proxy to any provider, and the decision layer that picks which bank, issuer and processor each creator lands on, with the rules owned by OnlyFans. | Best Case: Rappi (new provider implementation time cut to zero). | Outreach Angle: "Sagan has said the plan is financial products for creators and fewer problems with processors and banks. That is a vault question first: where every credential lives, and who decides where each creator's money goes."

**Insight #2: Several processors, no layer between them**
Evidence: Section 3 (audited accounts show receivables from several PSPs; Terms reserve the right to change providers; no orchestrator or vault vendor public). | Pain Point: each processor holds its own stored cards, so moving volume or replacing a provider risks re-collecting cards from fans on auto-renewing subscriptions. | Yuno Value Prop: one vaulted token that works on every processor connected, network tokens and account updater to keep it current, smart routing and failover; Yuno published figures of up to 7% approval uplift and 30% recovered revenue. | Best Case: Livelo (50% of failed transactions recovered, 5% approval uplift). | Outreach Angle: "Your Terms already allow you to change processors. The vault decides whether that is a configuration change or a card re-collection campaign."

**Insight #3: The payout side is where the scars are**
Evidence: Section 7 rows 2 and 4 (2021 bank crisis, CEO de-banked); Section 4A (USD-only payouts, wires outside the US, provider-caused delays); pending 7 days (21 in some countries) and 3 to 5 business days to pay (https://onlyfans.com/help/196/244/246 ; https://onlyfans.com/help/196/244/247). | Pain Point: $6.3B a year to creators moves on rails that a single bank decision can disrupt. | Yuno Value Prop: Banking Connectivity API (entities, onboarding on a named provider connection, accounts, ACH, same-day ACH, wire, RTP) and payouts on the same platform as the vault. | Best Case: inDrive (90% approval, new countries live within eight months on one integration). | Outreach Angle: "In 2021 the problem was the payout banks, not the checkout. A multi-bank design only works if the credentials to move a creator sit somewhere neutral."

**Insight #4: One third of revenue is outside the US, with entities only in the US and UK**
Evidence: Sections 1 and 2 ($587.9M outside the US; no entity in Germany, Canada, Spain, Mexico or Brazil; USD pricing with "currency conversion fees" borne by fans). | Pain Point: [INFERENCE, not confirmed] cross-border acquiring on non-US cards, with FX and issuer-decline costs. | Yuno Value Prop: local acquiring routes per market through the same integration, decided by rules on BIN and issuer country. | Best Case: Reserva (4% approval improvement). | Outreach Angle: only after Phase 1; OnlyFans scoped the vault to US balances first.

**Insight #5: 47 people run a $1.55B platform**
Evidence: Section 6 row 5. | Pain Point: any new payments layer must run by configuration, not headcount. | Yuno Value Prop: one API, one contract, rules editable without deployments, 24/7 Payments Concierge and a published SLA (y.uno/sla). | Outreach Angle: supporting point, not a lead.
