## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Waymo (Alphabet; $126B post-money after a $16B round in Feb 2026) is the US robotaxi leader, with 500K+ paid rides per week, 13 Waymo-app cities plus Austin and Atlanta on Uber, and a target of 1M rides per week by the end of 2026 [S1][S2][S3][S4]. Its payment stack is USD-only and card/wallet-centric (Apple Pay, Google Pay, the major card schemes, Waymo Cash and gift cards), and the processor is unnamed: the ToS says only "our payment processor" [S5][S6]. The decisive signal: until 5 Oct 2026 Waymo was hiring a Payments Partnerships lead to run "RFP selection" with "payment processors, acquirers, networks, and fintech partners" and to "launch Waymo's global payment infrastructure into new markets and territories" [S7]. London is pre-commercial, Tokyo is in testing, a German GmbH was registered and Toronto is announced for 2028 [S8][S9][S10][S11]. The Yuno opportunity is to be the multi-acquirer, multi-currency layer for that international build. A secondary angle is recovery on the new $29.99/month Premier subscription [S12].

**Analysis:** No public complaint volume surfaced, so don't pitch "your riders complain." The documented mechanics are the angle instead:
- **Auth plus re-auth on every route change.** This means many authorizations per trip, which feeds smart routing and approval rates (+7%).
- **ZIP/CVV-driven declines** show up with prepaid and international cards (Waymo accepts JCB and UnionPay, a tourist signal).
- **Simple retry on Premier.** The only recovery step is falling back to the rider's secondary saved card. That makes Premier a recovery and involuntary-churn opportunity (up to 50% transaction recovery).

## SECTION 10: Strategic Insights

**Insight #1: Waymo is selecting its global acquiring stack right now**
- **Evidence:** S3A/S6 #9. The job ran "RFP selection" with "processors, acquirers, networks", "first-of-its-kind commercial international agreements", and "local payment methods" [S7]. London, Tokyo, Germany and Toronto are in the pipeline [S8][S9][S1][S11].
- **Pain point:** Every new country means a new acquirer contract, a new integration, SCA/3DS, local methods and new reconciliation, all for a team built around USD.
- **Yuno value prop:** One API across local acquirers in each market, no-code PSP enablement, and a new market live in weeks rather than an integration project per country.
- **Best case:** InDrive (10 LATAM markets in under 8 months, 90% approval).
- **Outreach angle:** "As Waymo takes its payment infrastructure beyond USD, the hard part is running several acquirers per market without rebuilding each time. That layer is what we do."

**Insight #2: Premier creates a recurring-revenue recovery problem**
- **Evidence:** S5/S7. The only fallback when the default card fails is to try the rider's secondary saved card; if every card fails, the membership is paused or terminated [S12].
- **Pain point:** Involuntary churn on a $29.99/month membership that is just scaling past invite-only.
- **Yuno value prop:** Smart retries, cascading across acquirers and network tokens, up to 50% transaction recovery.
- **Best case:** Livelo (+5% approval, 50% recovery).
- **Outreach angle:** "Premier turns every failed card into a churned member. The recovery logic behind it is worth a look before the membership opens beyond invite-only."

**Insight #3: Many authorizations per trip and a diverse card mix**
- **Evidence:** S4/S5. A hold at hail, a re-auth on route change, ZIP/CVV declines, and JCB/UnionPay/Maestro acceptance (an international visitor mix) [S6].
- **Pain point:** Approval rates on holds and incremental auths, plus declines from foreign and prepaid cards.
- **Yuno value prop:** Smart Routing for a 7% approval uplift; BIN-based routing for foreign cards.
- **Best case:** Reserva (+4% approval in under 3 months).
- **Outreach angle:** "At 500K rides a week with a hold and re-auth on route changes, a few points of approval is real money."

**Insight #4: Fragmented money flows across channels**
- **Evidence:** S3A/S8. Waymo app, Uber as the checkout in Austin/Atlanta, Lyft in Nashville, Business corporate cards, Teen accounts, Waymo Cash, gift cards, and a Visa-only transit reward that Waymo "would like to expand to other payment processors" [S19][S21][S23][S25].
- **Pain point:** Network-specific features, reconciliation across many streams, and monitoring.
- **Yuno value prop:** Unified reconciliation and real-time monitors.
- **Best case:** Rappi (millisecond detection vs 5 to 10 minutes manually; 80% less analyst resolution time).
- **Outreach angle:** "Card-linked rewards on more than one network is a routing and data problem as much as a partnership one."
