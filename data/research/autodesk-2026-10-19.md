## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Autodesk (NASDAQ: ADSK; AutoCAD, Revit, Fusion, Maya) closed FY26 (Jan 31, 2026) at **$7.206B revenue, +18%**, with **64% international** [S2][S40]. The biggest payments fact is its go-to-market shift. Under the "new transaction model", partners quote and **Autodesk transacts and bills the customer directly**. Indirect revenue fell from **58% to 37%** in one year, so Autodesk now bills about **$4.56B (63%) itself**, up from about 42% [S2]. "Payment processing fees" now sit in its own opex [S2].

**The payment stack:**
- **Seller of record:** Autodesk's own entities sell in every region checked: Autodesk Inc for US and Canada, **Autodesk Ireland Operations for the EEA, Switzerland and the UK**, Autodesk India Online Store, and Autodesk Japan [S5][S6][S7][S8][S9].
- **Digital River:** older "DRI*Autodesk" statement descriptors point to Digital River as a past provider [S24]. No current page names it.
- **Card processor:** none is named anywhere.
- **Orchestrator:** none found.

**The Yuno opportunity:** Autodesk has just moved billions in collections from resellers onto its own rails, across 9+ store markets with local methods (SEPA, iDEAL, Boleto/Pix, konbini, Pay-easy, Zip, Plaid Pay by Bank) [S10 to S18]. On top of that it closed a **$3.6B MaintainX acquisition** in Aug 2026 [S37]. The pitch is authorization performance and renewal recovery on that new direct book, plus one layer to run local acquiring and methods per market.

**Trustpilot:** 2.2/5, but only 8 reviews (2 in the last 12 months), so the sample is too small to be a signal [S26].

**Autodesk's own failed-payment guidance [S21][S22]:**
- A failed charge is retried over several days.
- If the card is blocked, the customer must ask their bank to unblock it and approve automatic charges from Autodesk.
- Card details cannot be edited, only re-added.
- Name and address must match the issuer's records.
- Bank per-transaction or daily limits can block the charge.

**Analysis:** complaint volume is modest, but every pattern is a **renewal-path** problem:
- Issuer and AVS declines.
- Recovery that depends on the customer calling their bank.
- Card updates that create duplicate subscriptions.

This maps to Yuno's smart retries and routing (published: up to **7% authorization uplift, 30% recovered revenue**), network tokens and card lifecycle handling, and real-time monitors. The 63% direct-billing shift makes these renewal paths far bigger than they were when resellers collected. Use this in discovery, not as a cold-email accusation.

## SECTION 10: Strategic Insights

**Insight #1: Autodesk just became a $4.5B direct merchant**
- **Evidence:** Section 6 #1 and #2 show indirect revenue down from 58% to 37% and direct sales at about $4.56B [S2]. Section 7 #1 shows partners relieved of billing and collections [S32], and "payment processing fees" now sit in Autodesk's opex [S2].
- **Pain Point:** Collections, declines and processing cost that resellers used to absorb now sit with Autodesk. Each authorization point and basis point is now Autodesk's own P&L, at the same time as the margin-percentage pressure the agency model creates [S34].
- **Yuno Value Prop:** One layer over every acquirer and method, with smart routing (up to +7% authorization) and cost-based routing per market.
- **Best Case:** Rappi (zero implementation time for new providers, 80% less analyst resolution time).
- **Outreach Angle:** "With the transaction model now direct in most markets, your team owns authorization and collections on most of the book. That's the moment orchestration pays for itself."

**Insight #2: Renewals are the revenue, and recovery depends on the customer calling the bank**
- **Evidence:** Section 5 shows retries over several days, "ask your bank to unblock", cards that can't be edited [S22][S21], an AVS-style renewal failure that lapsed a subscription (Apr 2026) [S26], and card updates creating duplicate subscriptions [S27]. Subscription revenue is $6.74B of $7.21B [S40].
- **Pain Point:** Involuntary churn on monthly plans, which are direct-only, and on annual renewals.
- **Yuno Value Prop:** Smart retries and decline-code-aware routing (30% recovered revenue, published), network tokens and card lifecycle updates, plus real-time monitors on renewal batches.
- **Best Case:** Livelo (+5% approval, 50% recovery).
- **Outreach Angle:** Lead with "renewal recovery as you move monthly plans direct". Do not quote the complaints in cold outreach.

**Insight #3: Seller of record moved in-house, so the provider set is fragmented and post-migration**
- **Evidence:** Section 2 shows Autodesk entities as seller everywhere checked (Dec-2025 terms) [S5 to S9]. Section 3A shows a Digital River history [S24], a possible Adyen descriptor in AU [S24], CCAvenue (IN), Zip (AU), Plaid (US), PayPal and Citibank for invoices. UK and Canada are sold by non-local entities (Ireland, US) [S5][S7].
- **Pain Point:** Several providers and contracts, each with its own reporting, and possibly cross-border acquiring in the UK and Canada [INFERENCE, not confirmed].
- **Yuno Value Prop:** Local acquiring per market through one integration; switch or add acquirers without engineering work.
- **Best Case:** Reserva (+4% approval in under 3 months).
- **Outreach Angle:** Discovery question: "Since moving off a merchant-of-record model, how many acquirers are you running, and are UK and Canadian cards acquired locally?"

**Insight #4: LATAM is the fastest-growing region and already has local methods**
- **Evidence:** Section 1 shows Brazil and Mexico together at about 6.3% of traffic, and Other Americas revenue up 25% YoY, the fastest of any region [S1][S2]. Section 4A confirms Boleto/Pix and BRL/MXN checkout [S14][S15]. Entities exist in BR, MX, AR, CO, CR [S3].
- **Pain Point:** Getting LATAM approval rates and local methods right is hard: issuer behavior, installments, local acquiring [INFERENCE, not confirmed for Autodesk].
- **Yuno Value Prop:** LATAM-native coverage, with new markets live in weeks.
- **Best Case:** inDrive (10 LATAM markets in under 8 months, ~90% approval).
- **Outreach Angle:** Reference Boleto/Pix positively ("I saw you already offer Pix in Brazil"). Never claim a Mexico method gap.

**Insight #5: MaintainX and Payapps add billing stacks to integrate**
- **Evidence:** Section 6 #3 and #8: the $3.6B MaintainX acquisition closed Aug 3, 2026, and Payapps was bought for $387M [S37][S45].
- **Pain Point:** Separate PSP contracts and billing flows for acquired SaaS lines [INFERENCE, not confirmed].
- **Yuno Value Prop:** One orchestration layer to unify acquired businesses without re-platforming each one.
- **Best Case:** N/A (no public M&A consolidation case verified).
- **Outreach Angle:** Use as a second-touch email, not the opener.
