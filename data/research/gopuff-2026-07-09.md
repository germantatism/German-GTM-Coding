# SDR Research Brief — Gopuff (GoBrands, Inc.)
*Yuno Payment Orchestrator · Framework v8.0 · 2026-07-09*

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Gopuff** (legal parent GoBrands, Inc.; Philadelphia; instant-delivery / quick-commerce across 1,000+ US markets plus the UK) runs a **fragmented, multi-rail payment stack with no orchestrator**: a broad consumer pay-in mix (cards, PayPal, Cash App Pay, Apple/Google Pay, Venmo, SNAP EBT, FSA/HSA via Forage, gift cards, gated differently across app vs web and US vs UK), **Stripe on the driver-payout side (GoDrive)**, and card-present POS from the **BevMo!** alcohol-retail acquisition. The consumer acquiring PSP is not publicly disclosed. With a **new IPO-experienced CFO (Matt McBrady, Nov 2025)**, a fresh $250M raise at $8.5B, ~$4 contribution profit per order, and a likely IPO ahead, payment cost and authorization optimization are a board-level lever. The Yuno opportunity is **stack consolidation + smart routing + unified pay-ins/payouts** across a two-sided, multi-brand, US-plus-UK operation, not geographic expansion.
