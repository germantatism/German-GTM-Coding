## 9. Selling Yuno Here

**Core frame:** "You run at least four payment stacks for one customer base. Yuno makes them behave as one, without touching the billing systems, and pays for itself in recovered renewals and lower processing cost."

**Hooks with proof points (published Yuno cases only)**

- Recurring recovery: Livelo, +5% approval and 50% recovery. Published: up to 7% approval uplift, 30% recovered revenue.
- Multi-country LatAm in weeks: inDrive, 10 LatAm markets in under 8 months, 90% approval.
- Operations and monitoring: Rappi, zero implementation time, 80% less analyst resolution time; millisecond anomaly detection versus 5 to 10 minutes manually.
- Fast wins: Reserva, +4% approval in under 3 months.
- Cost: published up to 27% savings on payment costs.

**Landmines (do not say)**

- Any claim that a brand is missing a payment method.
- Anything about the Ergen family.
- Bankruptcy, the Hughes examiner allegations or the CEO exit, unless they raise it.
- Internal stats (subscriptions engine numbers) or internal names.
- Any price.
- Any claim of a native Recurly, CSG or Kenan connector until Solutions confirms it.
